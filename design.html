Design Requirements

How this site is designed

Colour scheme

Black backgrounds with hot-pink accents for buttons, links and highlights. “Waridi” is Swahili for rose, a nod to Kenya’s famous flower farms.

Typography

Serif headings for an elegant, hospitality feel and a clean sans-serif for body text.

Mobile responsive

Fluid grids, flexible type sizes and a CSS-only hamburger menu. Layouts adapt at 900px and 700px.

Technology

Pure HTML and CSS: no JavaScript, no frameworks, fast to load and easy to maintain.
